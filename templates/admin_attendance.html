Automatic handover

Operating model

People register

Registered person

Name, position, staff code, school, time in/out and online/offline source are retained against the person’s account.

Unregistered person

Reception captures a device token plus name, phone, gender, reason and time. The same device can be used to match the later checkout.

Offline operation

Reception devices queue scans locally and synchronize when connectivity returns.
