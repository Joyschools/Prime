# Prime Institution OS V17

V17 is the final pre-authentication polish build.

Highlights:
- Professional SVG notification and shortcut icons; no emoji notification bell.
- Lighter internal dashboard defaults with professional blue action controls.
- Desktop sidebar behavior retained for PC; mobile uses the left menu and touch-friendly scrolling.
- Sidebar groups and quick-access panels are constrained to the viewport and scroll their visible content into view.
- Reception is treated as a secretary/front-office workspace. On PC, Attendance Control is the first operational view; on mobile, Workspace is first and Reception Register is the scanner view.
- Reception has no Admin Command Centre link. Once authentication is enabled, Admin/ICT oversight and secretary privileges remain role-separated.
- Staff attendance schedule can be configured by Admin/ICT, and staff attendance CSV is available centrally.
- Staff Room provides institution-wide staff directory, online Jitsi meetings and duty assignment/notification tools.
- Personal dashboard appearance settings are available from My Space for background, accent, font and font size.
- Public landing-page settings remain separate from personal/internal workspace preferences.
- DEMO_AUTH_BYPASS remains enabled for the current presentation environment; authentication has intentionally not been reactivated.
